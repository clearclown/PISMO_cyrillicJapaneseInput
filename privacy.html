Privacy Policy

Pismo · 2026-09-29

Ordinary typing stays on your device

Pismo is a Japanese keyboard using Cyrillic, Arabic, Persian and Taiwanese Zhuyin characters. Phonetic conversion, dictionary lookup and Zenzai inference run on your device. Ordinary typing is not automatically transmitted. Pismo does not use advertising or tracking.

Core Japanese input works without an internet connection or Full Access.

Local settings, dictionaries and learning data

Settings, user dictionaries and learned conversion data are stored locally and shared between the app and keyboard through an App Group. Contact access and clipboard history are optional; when enabled, their contents are used locally for suggestions and related features.

Learning data and clipboard history can be cleared in the app settings. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data.

Optional reports and suggestions

If you choose to send a conversion report or word suggestion, selected words, readings and comments are sent to Google Forms. Depending on your selections in the confirmation screen, a submission may include surrounding text, an optional nickname, and app or device diagnostic information. A nickname or the submitted text may identify you.

Submissions are used to investigate problems and improve the dictionary and conversion. Review the contents before sending and avoid including personal information or other people's confidential information. Submitting a report is not required for normal Japanese input.

External services and deletion requests

Dictionary downloads and support pages or Google Forms that you open disclose connection information, such as an IP address, to the destination service. Those services have their own privacy policies.

For questions about submitted data or requests to delete a submission, contact clearcleaclown@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify the submission, such as its date or nickname.

Changes to this policy will be reflected in the text and update date on this page.
